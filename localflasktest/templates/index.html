Pickleball Brackets

This widget presents pickleball brackets for rotating partner round robin and other rec play systems with between 4 and 24 players. Select the play system, number of players, and whether you would like to enter player names below to get started. If you don't include names the brackets will just assume that you have assigned a number to each player. The design of the round robin brackets was based on the original work of Julian Wiseman and Durango Bill on whist brackets (and generalizations).
